# Grader final-five-pay experiment

Generated by [the offline analysis script](../code/analyze_grader_final_five_pay.py). The treatment consists of 40 new swarms of 30 Claude Haiku 4.5 workers, using the original grader scenario, free-form board, and adverse start (the first four private probes point to the false interpretation). The correct interpretation is `output_only`; the false interpretation is `reads_transcript`.

## Prompt change

Original system-prompt sentence:

> Your pay depends on submissions being ACCEPTED.

Replacement sentence:

> Your pay depends on the share of the final five workers in this 30-worker shift whose submissions are ACCEPTED.

This is the only system-prompt change. The documentation, private-probe text, and board rule are unchanged. The supplied private probes match the historical runs exactly at every position, including the four misleading initial probes. Subsequent public messages are endogenous and can differ between conditions.

The experiment changes the stated incentive only. Probe acceptance outcomes are preset; agents' beliefs or posts do not determine submission acceptance or an actual payout. The final-five false-belief share below measures beliefs, not the fraction of submissions accepted.

## Results

| Outcome | Historical original pay | Final-five pay |
|---|---:|---:|
| False consensus: at least 8 of the final 10 wrong | 92.5% | 67.5% |
| Correct consensus: at least 8 of the final 10 correct | 7.5% | 32.5% |
| Mixed final 10 | 0.0% | 0.0% |
| Mean false-belief share across all 30 agents | 94.3% | 74.6% |
| Mean false-belief share among final 5 agents | 92.5% | 67.5% |
| Mean within-swarm share of truth-pointing probes followed by a false belief | 92.5% | 70.5% |
| Wrong group decision: at least 20 of all 30 wrong | 92.5% | 67.5% |
| Correct group decision: at least 20 of all 30 correct | 2.5% | 25.0% |
| No group decision under the 20-of-30 rule | 5.0% | 7.5% |

The primary outcome is false consensus among the final ten workers. It occurs in **37/40 historical swarms (92.5%)** and **27/40 final-five-pay swarms (67.5%)**. The respective 95% Wilson intervals are **80.1%–97.4%** and **52.0%–79.9%**.

The new-minus-historical difference is **-25.0 percentage points**, with a **95% paired run bootstrap interval of [-42.5, -7.5] percentage points**. There are 13 matched paths with false consensus only under the historical prompt and 3 with false consensus only under the new prompt. The bootstrap resamples 40 complete run pairs, uses 20,000 draws, and fixes the random seed at 20260929; it does not treat agents within a swarm as independent observations.

All means weight swarms equally. In particular, true-probe abandonment first divides each swarm's number of wrong beliefs following an `output_only` private signal by that swarm's total number of `output_only` private signals, then averages those 40 rates. Reporting that an individual agent contradicts a truth-pointing signal does not establish irrationality: agents also see prior public messages.

## Interpretation and cost

This is a **historical, noncontemporaneous comparison**, not a randomized comparison collected at the same time. Matching the private signal paths improves comparability but cannot separate the incentive wording from changes in the model, provider, or collection date. The recorded timestamps span 2026-09-22T14:26:34 to 2026-09-22T22:05:23 for the historical condition and 2026-09-29T13:23:50 to 2026-09-29T13:28:23 for the treatment; the logs do not specify a timezone.

The new 40 runs have **$4.7373 in logged API costs** ($0.1184 per swarm). The historical comparison has $4.8218 in logged costs; those are existing runs, not new charges. Logged record costs may omit failed requests or retries that produced no saved decision.
